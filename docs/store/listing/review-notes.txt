Lustra lets you try new hair colours on a photo. There is no account, no login and no demo credentials. Everything works on first launch.

How to test:
1. Finish the short welcome screens. On Home, tap one of the sample photos (built into the app), or choose a photo from your library.
2. Wait a moment while the hair is detected on the device. Then open the colour tools, pick a colour, and try a style such as Ombre, Balayage or Highlights. Move the intensity, shine and brightness sliders. Drag the before and after slider.
3. Open the compare grid to see several colours at once, or use the refine brush to add or remove hair area.
4. Tap Save or Share. Before the export, the app offers one opt-in rewarded video ("Watch a short ad to export").

Ads: the app is free and supported by Google AdMob. A banner appears on Home only. Each export (Save to Photos, Save to Files or Share) offers one rewarded video that the user chooses to watch; a comparison grid counts as one export. If no ad is available (offline, no fill, consent pending), the export simply goes ahead without one. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no watermark. A Google UMP consent form may appear first in regions where it is required, followed by the App Tracking Transparency prompt; the app works the same whichever choice is made.

Permissions: Photos (read access through the system photo picker, add-only access to save results), Camera (only if the user taps to take a photo), Tracking (App Tracking Transparency, for ads). The app does not use the microphone or location.

Privacy: photos are processed on the device and are never uploaded. Hair detection uses an on-device model (MediaPipe hair segmenter, Apache-2.0) and Apple's Vision framework. The only network traffic is the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Privacy policy: https://ecomswiftllc.github.io/lustra/privacy/

Sample photos built into the app show hair from behind and are from Pexels (Pexels License); credits are in the project documentation and on https://ecomswiftllc.github.io/lustra/licenses/
