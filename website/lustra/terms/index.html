1. What Lustra is

Lustra is an app for iPhone and iPad that lets you try new hair colours on a photo. It finds the hair in the picture on your device and lets you try natural and fashion colours, ombre, balayage, highlights, two-tone and root shadow looks, adjust intensity, shine and brightness, cover gray, refine the result with a brush, compare before and after, compare several colours in a grid, save your favourite looks, and save or share the picture. Lustra shows how a colour could look. It does not change your real hair, and it does not offer hair styling, haircut or wig try-on.
