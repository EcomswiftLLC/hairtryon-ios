Common questions

How do I try a hair colour?
  1. Choose a photo, or one of the sample photos, on the Home screen. A photo of one person where the hair is clearly visible works best.
  2. Wait a moment while Lustra finds the hair on your device.
  3. Pick a colour, then pick a style: solid, ombre, balayage, highlights, two-tone or root shadow.
  4. Use the intensity, shine and brightness sliders to adjust it.
  5. Drag the before and after slider to check the change, then save or share.
The colour went onto the wrong place, or missed some hair

Use the refine brush. Choose Add to paint hair that was missed, or Remove to take colour off a spot that is not hair, such as a scarf or the background. You can undo any stroke. Photos that are sharp, evenly lit and show the hair clearly give the cleanest result. Hats, scarves, hoods, strong shadows and busy backgrounds make detection harder.

Lustra shows a hint about my photo

The app tells you when a photo is blurry, dark, very small, or has no clear face, and when a hat or scarf may cover the hair. You can still continue. If the photo has more than one person, choose the one you want to edit. For the best result, try a brighter photo with the hair in full view.

Why am I asked to watch an ad?

Lustra is free, and ads pay for it. Before you export, the app offers one short video that you choose to watch. One watched ad covers one export, and a comparison grid counts as one export. If you choose Not now, nothing is exported and you can try again any time. If no ad is available, for example when you are offline or your ad privacy choice does not allow ads, the export goes ahead without one. Editing never needs an ad.

Where are my saved looks?

Saved looks are kept on your device only. You can open them again on another photo and delete them in the app. If you delete the app, they cannot be recovered by us. Pictures you exported to Photos or Files are not affected. A full backup of your device may still contain the app's data if you made one before deleting it.

Can Lustra change my hairstyle or haircut?

No. Lustra changes the colour of hair that is already in your photo. It does not add bangs, curls, a haircut or a wig.

I saw an ad that seems wrong

Write to us at the address above. Tell us roughly when and where in the app you saw it and what it showed. A screenshot helps. We block ad categories and advertisers through Google AdMob where we can.

I want to change my ad or tracking choice

Open Settings in the app and look for the Ad privacy choices row, which appears where the law requires it. You can also change tracking in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, then Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier there.
